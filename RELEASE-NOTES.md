# Release Notes

All notable changes to Contact Manager are documented here, newest first.

---

## 1.0.0 — 2026-08-01

**First public release.** Contact Manager brings every contact from every source
into one place and helps you move, merge, and tidy your address book.

### Highlights
- 📇 **All your contacts in one list** — Google, Samsung, SIM, your phone, and app
  accounts (WhatsApp, Telegram, …) together, each tagged with where it lives.
- ➡️ **Move contacts to the right source** — decide which account each contact
  belongs in and move it there in a tap. Move one, or select a batch and move
  them all at once.
- 🧹 **Find & clean up duplicates** — automatically spot contacts that share a
  phone number, email, or a similar name, then **merge** or **link** them into a
  single clean entry.
- ✏️ **Edit anything** — names, phones, emails, company, job title, websites,
  addresses, birthdays, and more. Read-only app accounts are never overwritten.
- 📞 **SIM contacts** — read and edit the name + number entries stored on your SIM.
- 🔎 **Fast search**, swipe-to-delete, and multi-select for bulk move / merge /
  delete.

### Tablets
- 📱 **Adaptive layout** — on 7" and 10" tablets (and unfolded foldables) the app
  shows a navigation rail plus a two-pane list-detail view: your contact list on
  the left and the selected contact (or editor) on the right. Phones keep the
  familiar single-pane experience.

### Privacy
- Your **contacts stay on your device** — the app reads them directly from
  Android and never uploads your address book. No sign-up, no account.
- The app collects **anonymous usage analytics and crash reports** (no names,
  numbers, or emails) to help fix bugs and improve the experience.

### Good to know
- Moving a contact to a SIM keeps only the name and number a SIM can store, and
  always tells you before anything changes.
- Safe moves & merges: the app copies first and deletes second, so a mid-flight
  error can duplicate data but never loses it.
