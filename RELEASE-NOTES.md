# Release Notes

Play Store release notes ("What's new"), max **500 characters** per entry.

---

## 1.2 — 2026-10-02

Maintenance release: the crash-reporting and analytics libraries are up to date. Nothing changes in how the app works — your contacts stay on your device, as always.

---

## 1.1 — 2026-09-17

Move your contacts anywhere: export whole accounts, several at once, or just a selection to vCard files (photos included), and import vCard files into any account — SIM too — picking exactly which contacts to bring in. Copy contacts between accounts, even out of WhatsApp and other app-managed ones. Deleting across accounts now asks which ones to clear. Plus a new Settings screen with theme choice, and a search bar that tucks away while you scroll.

---

## 1.0.0 — 2026-08-01

Contact Manager 1.0 is here!

See every contact — Google, Samsung, SIM, your phone, and app accounts — together in one list. Move contacts to the right account, merge duplicates, and tidy up your address book. Edit contacts and SIM entries, search, and bulk move or delete. New two-pane layout on tablets. Your contacts stay on your device — nothing is uploaded.
