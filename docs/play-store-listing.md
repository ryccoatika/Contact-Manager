## Full description
*(Play limit: 4000 characters. This one: ~2,620.)*

```
Is your contact list a cluttered mess — the same person saved three times, duplicates after every sync, and contacts stuck in the wrong account? Contact Manager helps you move, merge, declutter, and tidy your entire address book — fast.

See every contact from every source in ONE list — Google, Samsung, your SIM card, this device, and app accounts like WhatsApp and Telegram — then put each one exactly where it belongs.

➡️ MOVE CONTACTS TO THE RIGHT SOURCE
This is what Contact Manager was built for. Decide which account each contact should live in, then move it there in a tap — pull contacts off your device or SIM into Google to back them up, split personal and work contacts into the right accounts, or consolidate everything into one place. Move a single contact, or select a whole batch and move them all at once. Moving to a SIM keeps just the name and number a SIM card can store, and it always tells you before anything changes.

✨ MORE YOU CAN DO
• 🧹 Declutter duplicates — automatically find contacts that share a phone number, email, or a similar name, then merge or link them into one clean entry.
• 📇 See where every contact lives — a clear tag shows which account each contact belongs to, so nothing is a mystery.
• 🔀 Merge and clean up — combine scattered copies of the same person without losing their details.
• 📞 Manage SIM contacts — read and edit the name + number entries stored on your SIM card.
• 🗑️ Quick cleanup — swipe to delete, or select many contacts at once to move, merge, or remove in a single tap.
• 🔎 Fast search — find anyone instantly by name or number.

🎯 BUILT FOR DECLUTTERING YOUR CONTACTS
Whether you're moving everything into one Google account, sorting contacts between personal and work, clearing duplicates after a messy import, or tidying up before switching phones — Contact Manager gets your address book neat and organized again.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your contacts stay on your device. The app reads directly from Android's contacts — no sign-up, no account, and it never uploads your address book to the cloud. Read-only accounts (like WhatsApp) are never modified; they can only be copied or linked, never overwritten.

⭐ WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
• Clean, modern, easy-to-use design.
• No login. No ads.
• Works across all your contact sources at the same time.
• Safe moves and merges — it copies first and deletes second, so you never lose data.

Move your contacts to the right account, remove duplicates, and finally get a tidy, organized address book. Download Contact Manager today.
```

  `Contact Manager: Move & Merge` or `Move Contacts & Merge Dupes`.
- Keep the first ~3 lines of the full description punchy — that's all users see
  before "read more".

  term to a natural frequency.
